Glaciers

I study how the world’s glaciers respond to a changing climate. Methodologically, I draw on Bayesian inference and inverse modeling. I’m also interested in surrogate modeling of complex physical systems. Of course, having a background in statistics/machine learning, I am interested in anything to do with machine learning and broader artificial intelligence as well, not just limited to glaciology (although that is the main interest)!
